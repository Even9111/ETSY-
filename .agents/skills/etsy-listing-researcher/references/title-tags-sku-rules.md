# Etsy title, tags, and SKU rules

## Title

Target 135–140 English characters inclusive. Put the main product phrase early and use the remaining verified space for material, motif, function, style, and recipient/use-case terms. Do not repeat a word just to reach 135 characters.

Before delivery, count the exact final string, including spaces, commas, hyphens, and numbers.

## Tags

Return exactly 13 tags. Count each tag independently, including spaces. Keep every tag at 20 characters or fewer. Use a portfolio of distinct intents:

- core product;
- material/construction;
- motif/style;
- function/use case;
- audience or gift context when relevant.

Do not use unsupported material terms or `personalized` when the item has no personalization option.

## SKU naming

SKU is an internal variant identifier, not an SEO phrase. Build it from visible attributes:

`[PRODUCT/FAMILY]-[MOTIF or COLOR]-[HARDWARE or VERSION]`

Examples:

- `WRIST-FLORAL-BLK`
- `WRIST-FLORAL-PURP`
- `PHONE-RUFFLE-PINK`
- `BAG-PEARL-IVORY`

Use uppercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. Keep each SKU unique and no longer than 20 characters. Never encode an attribute that is not visible or confirmed in the product data.
